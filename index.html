Cybersecurity Basics

A short guide to what cybersecurity is, why it matters and who is working to keep us safe online.

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is about protecting computers, networks and the information stored on them from people who should not have access. It covers the hardware, the software and the data, and it applies to everyone from big companies to a student with a laptop.

The CIA Triad

Security teams often use a simple model called the CIA triad. It has nothing to do with the spy agency. It is a way to check if information is really protected. If one of the three parts fails, the data is not secure.

The Three Parts

Confidentiality

Only the people who are allowed to see the data can see it. Passwords and encryption help with this.

Integrity

The data stays accurate and has not been changed by someone without permission. A changed bank balance would be an integrity problem.

Availability

The systems and data can be used when people need them. An attack that knocks a website offline breaks availability.
